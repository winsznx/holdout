# Thesis

Traders do not lack rules. Nothing checks them.

Holdout turns a Bitget trader's own fills into a rulebook proven in dollars. Each rule is tested on trades it never saw, kept only if it beats a same-size random rule, and then every new trade idea is checked against it. The trader makes every decision. Holdout never places an order.

GetAgent 2.0 and journal apps describe the past. Holdout tests a fix out of sample, and says "no rule needed" when nothing beats chance.

What the evidence can and cannot show: Bitget Demo bots with one planted habit each test whether the method finds the habit and stays quiet on a disciplined control. They say nothing about whether Holdout improves real traders. That claim needs human histories and is made only if the pre-registered test passes on them.
